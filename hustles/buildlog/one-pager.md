# Hustle: Build-in-public posts about Compound City

**Stage:** dropped 2026-10-26 (LinkedIn's terms ban automated posting, so it would need Vivek to post by hand; see `hustles/OPERATING.md`) · **Proposed:** 2026-10-13

## What it is
A post every two weeks on LinkedIn (and optionally Medium) about building an AI-run trading lab: the risk engine, the paper-testing tournament, the city, and honest results, including setups that fail.

## Who pays and why
Almost nobody, directly. This is the weakest money-maker on the list and the strongest resume piece. It shows recruiters (finance and supply-chain internships included) real systems work under Vivek's name, and it gives the template and Pine hustles an audience to sell to.

## Money
- Start cost: **$0**.
- **Realistic direct income in 90 days: $0 to $25. Confidence: high that it stays small.** The value is indirect: audience for the other hustles, and resume material.

## Who does what
- **Claude:** drafts each post from real STATE.md and DECISIONS.md entries, with screenshots of the city.
- **Vivek:** edits for his own voice, approves, and posts it himself.
- **Your hours:** about 1 a week.

## Rules
- Never show tokens, account numbers or personal details.
- Never present paper results as live, and no performance hype.
- Say plainly that Claude assists with the build.

## First 3 steps
1. Claude drafts post #1: "I built the risk engine before the strategy."
2. Vivek picks the platform (LinkedIn recommended for the resume).
3. Vivek approves and posts it.

## Kill rule
None for money. Pause it if posts take more than 1 hour a week of Vivek's time.
