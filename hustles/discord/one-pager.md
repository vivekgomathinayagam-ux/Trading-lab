# Hustle: Discord bots for trading communities

**Stage:** dropped 2026-10-26 (client work needs live conversations, server permissions and deadlines with a person; see `hustles/OPERATING.md`) · **Proposed:** 2026-10-13

## What it is
Set-up-and-maintain bots for small trading Discord servers: TradingView alert relays (webhook to channel), daily market recaps, and role or welcome helpers. Vivek's existing recap bot for Bird Gang is the portfolio piece (shown only with the server owner's OK).

## Who pays and why
Server owners who want automation but can't build it. A comparable Fiverr gig ("Simple Alert Relay") is priced at $150 with 3-day delivery. Maintenance or hosting can add $10 to $25 a month per client.

## Money
- Start cost: **$0**. Sold through the same Fiverr account as Pine work (Fiverr keeps 20%). Hosting goes on the client's own account, so it never costs us.
- **Realistic first 90 days: $0 to $450 (zero to three small jobs). Confidence: low.**

## Who does what
- **Claude:** writes and tests the bot, writes setup docs and a case study, drafts client messages.
- **Vivek:** talks to clients, adds the bot to their server with their permission, approves each delivery.
- **Your hours:** 3 to 4 a week while a client is active.

## Risks
- Finding clients takes outreach. Only through marketplaces or communities Vivek is in, with permission. No spam.
- Discord's bot rules and client data privacy apply.
- No signal-selling bots with performance claims.

## First 3 steps
1. Claude packages a demo bot and a one-page case study.
2. Vivek adds a Discord bot gig to the Fiverr profile.
3. Vivek approves the gig going live.

## Kill rule
After 90 days with no paid job, retire it.
