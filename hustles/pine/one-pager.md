# Hustle: Pine Script commissions

**Stage:** proposed (waiting on Vivek's yes at City Hall) · **Proposed:** 2026-10-13 · **Autonomy:** medium

## What it is
Custom TradingView indicators and strategies coded to a buyer's rules, sold as a Fiverr gig under Vivek's name. Vivek already ships Pine Script v6 work (CVD turn indicator, ORB), so there is a real portfolio to show.

## Who pays and why
Traders who have rules but can't code them. Typical gigs start low: one active seller charges $10 for a simple 1-2 condition indicator, or $20 an hour. Bigger jobs (multi-condition strategies with alerts) run $50 to $200.

## Money
- Start cost: **$0**. Fiverr keeps 20% of each order.
- **Realistic first 90 days: $0 to $300. Confidence: low to medium.** New sellers often wait weeks for a first order, and the low end is crowded.

## Who does what
- **Claude:** writes, tests and debugs the Pine code; drafts the gig page, every client reply and delivery notes; builds 2-3 portfolio indicators.
- **Vivek:** owns the Fiverr account, sends every client message (Claude drafts them), checks each delivery on TradingView before submitting, and answers on time (response time affects Fiverr ranking).
- **Your hours:** 3 to 5 a week while orders are active.

## Rules this has to follow
- Fiverr allows AI-assisted work, but the freelancer is fully accountable for it. Work must be customized to the client, never generic output, and if a client asks for no AI, the workflow has to be disclosed.
- TradingView forbids unrealistic or unsubstantiated performance claims, and pricing can't be advertised in script descriptions. No cold DMs or comment spam.

## Risks
Slow start; scope creep (fixed by clear package limits); a bad review early hurts ranking; income is taxable.

## First 3 steps
1. Claude drafts the gig page and 3 packages ($15 / $45 / $120), plus 2 portfolio indicators.
2. Vivek creates the Fiverr seller profile.
3. Vivek approves and publishes the gig.

## Kill rule
After 90 days, if net income is under $50 or it costs more than 4 hours a week per $50 earned, retire it.
