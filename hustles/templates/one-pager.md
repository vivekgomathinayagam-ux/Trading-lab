# Hustle: Trader spreadsheet templates

**Stage:** building, from 2026-10-26 (autonomous department; the only step for Vivek is the one-time store and payout setup once a product is ready) · **Proposed:** 2026-10-13

## What it is
Google Sheets templates for options day traders, built from what Vivek actually uses:
1. A trade journal with R-multiple stats, win rate, expectancy, and a calendar view.
2. An options risk calculator: max loss and breakeven for verticals and 0DTE, plus a position-size calculator from a % risk rule.
Sold as a digital download on Gumroad (direct link), and optionally on Etsy.

## Who pays and why
Retail options traders who want structure but don't want to build spreadsheets. Demand exists: one Google Sheets trading journal on Etsy sells at $9 on sale ($15 list) from a shop with 8.9k total sales. The same evidence shows the market is crowded.

## Money
- Start cost: **$0**. Gumroad has no monthly fee. Etsy charges $0.20 per listing if we add it.
- Price: $12 for the pair.
- Fees: Gumroad direct sales cost 10% + $0.50, plus card processing of about 2.9% + $0.30, so roughly $9.65 is kept per $12 sale. Sales through Gumroad's own marketplace (Discover) cost 30%. Etsy charges 6.5% per transaction plus payment processing.
- **Realistic first 90 days: $0 to $150. Confidence: low.** Building the product is easy; getting found with no audience is the hard part.

## Who does what
- **Claude:** builds the sheets, makes screenshots, writes listing copy and the FAQ, ships updates, drafts buyer replies, and logs every sale and fee in `ledger/money.csv`.
- **Vivek:** creates the Gumroad (and optional Etsy) account in his name, connects the payout bank account, approves each listing before it goes live, and sends buyer replies.
- **Your hours:** about 2 hours of setup, then about 30 minutes a week.

## Risks
- Crowded market. Some refund requests are normal.
- Etsy now expects sellers to disclose AI involvement. Listings will say the templates were built with AI assistance.
- No performance claims. A journal doesn't make anyone profitable, and the copy will say so.
- Income is taxable. Vivek handles taxes; the ledger tracks income and fees.

## First 3 steps
1. Claude builds v1 of both sheets (no approval needed; nothing goes public).
2. Vivek creates the Gumroad account and connects payout.
3. Vivek approves the listing; Claude publishes the copy at $12.

## Kill rule
After 60 days live, if sales minus fees are below $20 total, retire it.
