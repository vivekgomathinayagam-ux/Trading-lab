## Short description
A trade journal that works out your win rate, expectancy, profit factor and drawdown for you, plus a calculator that tells you the max loss, breakeven and position size of an options trade before you place it.

## Description
**Journal**: type each trade (symbol, entry, exit, contracts, fees, planned risk). It fills in P&L, R-multiple, win/loss, running P&L and drawdown. Tabs: Stats (win rate, average win/loss, payoff ratio, expectancy in $ and R, profit factor, best/worst, max drawdown, streaks, equity chart), By Setup (results per setup tag), Calendar (green and red days for any month).

**Risk & Position Size Calculator**: set your account size and the % you allow yourself to lose per trade and per day. Then check a long call/put or a vertical spread (bull call, bear put, bull put, bear call): max loss, max profit, breakeven, reward-to-risk, whether it fits your limit, and the most contracts that fit.

**Made for** SPY/QQQ-style options day traders who want a record and a rule, not another signal service.

## What it is not
Not a signal, not advice, and it does not make anyone profitable. It is arithmetic and record-keeping on the numbers you type. Options trading involves risk of loss. Sample rows are made up; delete them before you start.
