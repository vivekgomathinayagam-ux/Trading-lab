# What to Track in an Options Trading Journal Spreadsheet (free checklist + worked example)

Arithmetic and record-keeping only. Not advice, not a signal, no prediction of results. Options involve risk of loss. Written with AI assistance (Claude). The example trades are made up.

## The 12 columns that matter

1. Date and time in/out. 2. Underlying and contract (e.g. SPY 570 call, or a 570/575 put spread). 3. Direction and structure (long, debit spread, credit spread). 4. Entry price and exit price per contract. 5. Contracts. 6. Fees (commissions and regulatory fees, both ways). 7. **Planned max loss in dollars** (this is your "1R"). 8. Net P&L in dollars after fees. 9. **R-multiple** = net P&L / planned max loss. 10. Setup name (one label per rule you trade). 11. Followed the plan? (yes/no). 12. One line on what happened.

Columns 7, 9 and 11 are the ones most journals skip, and they are what let you judge a setup instead of a day.

## Worked example (10 made-up trades, each risking $80)

| # | Net P&L | R |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +120 | +1.50 |
| 2 | -80 | -1.00 |
| 3 | -80 | -1.00 |
| 4 | +200 | +2.50 |
| 5 | -80 | -1.00 |
| 6 | +60 | +0.75 |
| 7 | -80 | -1.00 |
| 8 | +150 | +1.88 |
| 9 | -80 | -1.00 |
| 10 | +90 | +1.13 |

Computed by script from the rows above:

- Trades 10, net +$220, win rate 50%.
- Average win +$124, average loss -$80, profit factor 1.55 (gross wins 620 / gross losses 400).
- Average R per trade (expectancy) +0.275R, about +$22 per trade.
- Starting from $1,000: ending equity $1,220, **max drawdown $160** (peak $1,120 down to $960), longest losing streak 2.

## How to read it

- Ten trades is far too few to trust. Treat results as meaningful only after dozens of trades per setup, and compare against doing nothing.
- If "followed the plan = no" trades are your biggest losses, the fix is the rules, not the setup.
- Track fees: on small option trades they can be a visible share of the planned risk.
