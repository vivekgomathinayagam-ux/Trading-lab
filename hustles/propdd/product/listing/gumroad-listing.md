# Listing draft: Funded-Account Drawdown Tracker ($9)
**Name:** Funded Account Drawdown Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets)
**Summary:** Log your daily P&L and see your drawdown floor, dollars of cushion and daily-loss use against your own firm's rules.
**Description:**
Know how close you are to breaching before you place the next trade. Enter your challenge rules once (account size, profit target, daily loss limit, max drawdown, trailing end-of-day or static, whether the floor stops rising at the starting balance), then add one row per day with that day's net P&L. The workbook calculates balance, highest end-of-day balance, drawdown floor, cushion in dollars and percent, daily-loss used, progress to target and a plain status (OK / Caution / Breached).
Includes: Start here guide, Rules and live status tab, Daily log (200 rows) with sample data to delete.
Good to know: firms differ (intraday trailing, equity-based daily loss, etc.). This models end-of-day versions only; your firm's dashboard is the authority. Not affiliated with any firm. It is arithmetic on numbers you type, not advice or a signal; trading involves risk of loss. Built with AI assistance (Claude) and tested by recalculating every formula against hand calculations.
