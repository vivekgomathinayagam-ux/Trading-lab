# Hustle: Funded-Account Drawdown Tracker (experiment 2)
**Stage:** building (file built and recalculated 2026-10-29; listing is browser-only work, queued) · **Price:** $9 · **Start cost:** $0

## What it is
An Excel/Google Sheets workbook for traders in funded-account challenges. Type the firm's rules once (start balance, target, daily loss, max drawdown, trailing or static, floor locks at start or not), log each day's P&L, and it shows balance, highest end balance, drawdown floor, dollars of cushion, daily-loss used and a status line.

## Who pays and why
Traders in prop-firm challenges, where one breached rule ends the account. Evidence of demand: free and paid tools already exist for exactly this (a Gumroad "consecutive loss based risk calculator" at paulfx.gumroad.com, a "prop firm drawdown calculator" at onetradejournal.com, a Notion prop-firm tracker at traderssecondbrain.com, and TradeZella publishes a "vs spreadsheets" page for prop-firm tracking), so people search for it, and the market is crowded. This one differs by being a simple offline workbook with no subscription.

## Honesty limits
No pass-the-challenge or profit claims. States that firms differ and only end-of-day models are covered. Not affiliated with any firm. AI-assisted disclosure in the listing. Does not name or imitate any firm.

## Channel
