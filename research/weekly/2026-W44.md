| Q2.1 Price moves toward the biggest positive 0DTE node (null 50%) | 47.9% [37.8, 58.0], n=94 | 51.1% [41.0, 61.2] |

| Q2.3 Range, within 0.25% of the biggest negative node minus not | −0.079 [−0.180, +0.013] | −0.116 [−0.230, −0.004] |
- No magnet, no gamma-regime effect on range. Q2.3 points the **opposite** way from the hypothesis (near a big negative node = smaller range) and only one of 6 heat tests has a CI that barely excludes zero: with 8 tests that is what chance looks like. Not a finding.
- Tempest, 522 days per symbol (Oct 2024-Oct 2026), split in halves:
  | | Next-day \|move\| > priced 1-sigma (normal ≈ 31.7%) | mean \|move\| / priced |
  |---|---|---|
  | SPY, 1st half / 2nd half | 21.1% / 23.4% | 0.67 / 0.69 |
  | QQQ, 1st half / 2nd half | 24.5% / 28.4% | 0.69 / 0.75 |
  For a normal distribution the mean would be 0.80, so the priced move is about 15% too wide on average, in both halves and both symbols. Term slope (svx9 − svx30; positive = backwardation) does not change the breach rate (CIs straddle 0 and flip sign between halves).
- Reading: the board itself isn't telling us where price goes intraday. The usable fact is the variance premium: short defined-risk premium around the priced move wins more often than the price implies. Whether it pays after bid/ask and the fat tail is the question. That needs real option prices (Skylit has 30 days intraday) and is Wednesday's item, pre-registered first.
